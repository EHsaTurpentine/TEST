CUTTING SEASON

  • SCORE the ice — walk your horse team over frozen floes to score them.
  • BREAK & HAUL — your crew pries scored floes into BLOCKS. Grab one, then rush it to an ICE HOUSE.
  • SWIM CAREFULLY — dark water kills instantly. Lighter water is crossable but drains your SWIM meter; run dry and you freeze.
  • WATCH THE RIVAL (blue) — they scavenge your blocks to build BARRICADES across the swim lanes. Lean into one to chip it down; breaking it frees the block inside.
  • 5 STAGES — more houses, more crew, more chaos each time.
Move: Arrow Keys / WASD
