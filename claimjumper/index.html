CLAIMJUMPER

  • CLIMB the claim — work your way up ladders and platforms to the stake at the top.
  • DODGE the defenders — Rock-Throwers and Archers lob and loose at you; Prospectors chuck gems just as hard. Getting hit costs a life.
  • WATCH FOR VINEGAR — the Miner/Soldier's thrown bottle leaves a puddle that hurts just as much as a direct hit.
  • 5 CLAIMS — Mountain Vista, Rocky Cliff, Esmeralda, Pine Grove, Junction Bar. Tougher defenders join in as you go.
Move: Arrow Keys / WASD   Climb: Up/Down on a ladder
